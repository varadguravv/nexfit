THE FUNDAMENTALS

HOW MUSCLE GROWS

Muscle development requires an appropriate training stimulus, adequate recovery and progressive overload.

Hypertrophy Principles

Progressive resistance training creates the stimulus for muscle adaptation. Consistent training combined with adequate recovery supports continued development.

  • Progressive overload: Gradually increase the training challenge.
  • Controlled repetitions: Maintain technique and control throughout the movement.
  • Training volume: Accumulate enough productive work without allowing fatigue to overwhelm recovery.
  • Recovery: Allow trained muscles sufficient time to recover before demanding another hard session.

The Recovery Rule

  • Smaller groups: approximately 24–48 hours.
  • Arms: approximately 48 hours.
  • Large muscle groups: approximately 48–72 hours.
  • Important: actual recovery depends on training volume, intensity, sleep, nutrition and individual factors.

RECOVERY IS TRAINING

YOU DON'T GROW WHILE YOU TRAIN.

Training provides the stimulus. Recovery allows your body to adapt. Sleep, nutrition, hydration and appropriate rest between demanding sessions all contribute to your ability to train consistently.

```
```
TRAIN SMART

PROTECT THE PROGRESS.

Good training isn't simply about pushing harder. Technique, appropriate progression and recovery matter.

01

TECHNIQUE FIRST

Learn the movement before chasing heavier loads. Controlled repetitions create a stronger foundation.

02

PROGRESS GRADUALLY

Increase training difficulty progressively rather than making sudden jumps in volume or intensity.

03

RESPECT PAIN

Persistent or significant pain should not be ignored. Stop aggravating movements and seek appropriate professional assessment when needed.
